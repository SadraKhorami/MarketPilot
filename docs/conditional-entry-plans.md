# Conditional AI entry plans

New research plans carry `conditionalEntry.version = 2`; existing JSON plans remain readable without fabricating new rules or news evidence. Preferences have an optional JSON entry policy. Existing preferences preserve their manual timeframe with the former higher-timeframe mapping. New preferences default to Auto. Apply migration `20261004120000_conditional_entry` before using the updated API. Prisma Client must be generated and normal package builds performed by the deployment workflow; the local migration is applied.

## Supported proposals

Auto proposes entry and trend intervals, scenario, condition and validity. The server rejects missing Auto selections and validates the proposal before exposing a plan. Scalp permits 1m/5m entry, day 5m/15m/1h, swing 1h/6h, hybrid 5m/15m/1h/6h. A trend interval must be strictly higher than entry. Manual choices override AI choices. Holding style does not close or expire executed trades.

Breakout uses the preceding twenty completed-candle range boundary. Pullback requires an observed break of the earlier range (the forty-four candles preceding the recent six). Stops use recent swing extremes plus a quarter ATR; targets are observed structural levels, ordered beyond Entry. Both timeframes require sufficient, fresh completed candles, a continuous recent window and aligned moving-average context. Candles must have valid OHLC ordering. Closed markets, stale prices, invalid level ordering, excessive stop distances, missing targets and entries too far from current price produce an unavailable reason.

Supported entry rules are a range touch (pullback only), a completed close beyond Entry for breakout, a retest of Entry followed by a close on the breakout side for pullback, and a completed break followed by a separate later retest candle that touches Entry and closes on the breakout side. OHLC never proves intrabar ordering. Reversal rules are unavailable. A daily entry has no supported higher interval and is unavailable for a new conditional plan.

Entry validity is an explicit count of one to twelve entry candles, capped at seventy-two hours, with an exact deadline and stored reason. New close/retest proposals store `validityBasis = entry_candle_closes`: the count means future entry-timeframe close boundaries from proposal, including the current forming candle's future close. For UTC-aligned candles, a proposal at 19:34 with three 15m closes ends at 20:15 UTC. New proposals store the observed candle-grid offset; providers with shifted sessions retain their actual boundaries. Legacy tracking derives that grid from the supplied confirmation candles and pauses if it is unknown, without changing the stored deadline. Touch proposals store `elapsed_duration`, measured from proposal. Plans without this field retain their original elapsed deadline exactly; no saved plan is migrated or rewritten. Saving starts monitoring but never restarts or extends the deadline. A confirmation close exactly at the deadline is eligible; a later close is not. Validity is independent of holding duration. Distance is capped at one and a half ATR; chasing a breakout more than a quarter ATR beyond the entry area is rejected. Pullback proposals may wait for price to return to Entry within the distance bound. These are deterministic guardrails, not calibrated performance probabilities.

Operational rationale, validity text and new stage values derive from validated executable fields. Model proposal prose cannot add invalidation rules. A breakout close on the wrong side of Entry is an unmet trigger, not invalidation; price-based invalidation requires the stored Stop touched before entry. The stored economic and capital checks can independently reject a confirmation fill. Both explicit directions and Auto proposals must align with completed entry and trend timeframe moving-average context. Existing free-text model reasons remain in their audit JSON; the UI describes their stored executable condition rather than displaying contradictory reasons as instructions.

## Evidence, confirmation and history

Every explicit setup request retrieves relevant stored news before model planning. Availability, latest publication, retrieval time and a twenty-four-hour freshness rule are preserved. Missing or stale news is disclosed rather than replaced with invented events. No economic-calendar connection, bid/ask spread, broker commissions or executable liquidity exists in the present observation contract; the plan declares these unavailable.

Sizing uses manually supplied capital and the cost-inclusive stop risk, subject to the cash budget described below. Currency conversion is not invented; sizing is unavailable when account and quote differ. Open risk uses recorded Journal positions and their remaining quantities in the matching quote; that overview measures price distance to stored stops and excludes fees. Unknown, estimated and other-currency positions are counted as unknown. Capital is per proposal and does not implement a shared wallet or reserve funds across positions.

Before saving, the API rechecks matching fresh data, entry distance, invalidation and deadline. It saves the server proposal and confirmed context, not browser-supplied levels. Generic editing cannot mutate a confirmed v2 plan's levels, timeframes, conditions or expiry. Chat edits create a fresh proposal. A confirmed replacement atomically cancels only the prior unexecuted waiting plan, retains its original data, and creates links in both timelines. Plan-content hashes protect revisions without treating routine monitor updates as plan edits. Open trades cannot be replaced.

AI plans are automatic simulations. Entry opens when the saved condition is observed; completed one-minute candles establish touch and SL/TP ordering, while close/retest confirmations use the saved analysis timeframe. Entry expiry affects waiting simulations only. Ambiguous ordering inside a one-minute candle and unavailable historical coverage pause tracking without inventing fills. TP allocations split the supplied quantity equally by default; new plans use their explicit cost estimate. Without capital, reference-unit fills measure return percentages while quantity remains null and monetary analytics exclude the simulation. Manual reference exits take a percentage of the remaining position and preserve estimated costs. No brokerage integration is involved. Historical observation events remain in the audit timeline after an explicitly recorded simulation repair.

Tracking retains the boundary minute until its completed entry-timeframe candle is available. A cached forming confirmation can therefore be retried without skipping a possible entry or inferring expiry. One-minute break/retest entries record a completed break before considering a separate later retest. A partial start minute cannot prove that a retest happened after saving. Entry/breakout events identify the actual confirmation timeframe, while touch and exit events identify the tracking timeframe; an ordinary close entry never claims that another retest is required.

## Journal execution integrity

Unchanged date-picker values retain their original seconds and milliseconds. User-edit versions exclude routine monitor timestamps but include actual trade fields and exits. Manual corrections preserve partial exits, prevent reverting an executed position to an unexecuted plan, and keep estimated fills labelled as estimates when only notes change. Exit fees are separate from entry fees; realized results allocate entry fees to the exited quantity. Position value can be entered in quote currency and converted using the entered execution price without rounding unchanged quantities.

Confirmed AI executions have a separate actual-entry editor that preserves the original proposal. Replacing a closed estimated result requires explicit actual entry and exit details. Earlier observations remain in the timeline. Monitoring uses execution/revision-specific event keys, refuses stale snapshots of concurrently edited plans, and pauses when partial-candle timing or missing coverage makes an outcome unknowable.

## Economic quality checks

New AI proposals require an explicit user estimate for combined entry/exit fees, spread and slippage, expressed as a percentage of entry position value. Unknown costs never become zero. An explicitly entered zero is permitted for a cost-free simulation. These assumptions are stored with the proposal; they are not verified execution quotes.

Every target must remain profitable after estimated costs. Reward is weighted by the saved target allocations (equal shares with the remainder on the final target), and the net reward / stop risk must meet the user minimum, default 1.5R and never below 1R. Stop risk includes the same estimated round-trip costs. Structural targets and stops are preserved; failed plans are rejected rather than adjusted to manufacture a ratio. The save endpoint repeats these checks and refuses old proposals that lack the new quality assessment.

Sizing uses the smaller of the risk-budget size and the cash-affordable size including costs. Capital is a manually supplied per-proposal budget, not a synchronized portfolio balance. A candle-confirmed entry is rechecked at its actual simulated fill; a weakened ratio or exceeded budget cancels the waiting plan without opening a position. Leverage is unsupported.

Automatic exits allocate the remaining estimated costs proportionally to exit quantity. The round-trip estimate already includes entry fees: any separately recorded entry fees reduce the estimated exit budget, preventing double charging. Recorded fees above that budget remain actual costs. Correcting the entry price, size or fees recalculates estimated exit costs; recorded exit fees are preserved. Supplying an amount later rescales reference quantities and costs. Historical trades without a stored estimate retain their original assumptions.

## Consistent research evidence

AI research indicators, entry validation and both monitoring modes use completed candles. Completion is bounded by evaluation time, snapshot fetch time and provider request start, so a cached unfinished candle cannot become final just because time advances or a response crosses the interval boundary. Provider and cache boundaries validate requested market, provider symbol, interval, source, OHLC ordering and timestamps. Live observed prices remain separate from the latest completed close. Calculation history is included in the bounded model evidence independently of the chart window.

Trade target candidates and AI swing levels use observed wick pivots, confirmed by two completed candles on each side, across the full available calculation history. A later completed close above a resistance pivot or below a support pivot invalidates it in that role; wick touches alone do not. No automatic role reversal is invented. Close-price percentiles remain descriptive chart statistics and are not used as trade targets. Nearest target candidates are not skipped to inflate reward/risk; the existing cost and weighted-exit checks still apply. A confirmed historical level is evidence of a past pivot, not a guarantee of future support or resistance.

Asset news filters match complete names/tickers or explicit asset tags. A substring such as SOL inside “oversold” is not relevant Solana coverage. Empty relevant coverage remains unavailable. Automatic interpretation language resolves to Persian for Persian questions and English otherwise; explicit language preferences take precedence.
